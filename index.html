Before you begin

  • One question is shown at a time. Use Previous and Next to move between questions; your answers are kept.
  • Use Flag Question to mark a question you want to come back to. Flagging does not change your answer.
  • Open the Question Navigator to jump to any question by number.
  • On Question 50, Next becomes Review & Submit. You can check every answer before you submit.
  • The 90-minute timer starts when you press Start Test and cannot be paused. The test submits itself when time runs out.
  • Each correct answer earns 2 marks. Wrong or blank answers earn 0. You need 70 marks to pass.
  • Correct answers and the KOC standard behind each one are shown only after you submit.
